Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what information buisinesstoday ("we", "us") collects when you visit buisinesstoday.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. We process personal data in line with applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.
  • Technical information. Our hosting provider automatically records standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and time of request) to keep the site secure and working.
  • Analytics and advertising. If we add analytics or advertising services (for example Google Analytics or Google AdSense), those services may use cookies to measure visits and show ads. We will update this policy before enabling them.

How we use information

  • To reply to your messages.
  • To operate, secure and improve the website.
  • To understand which content is useful to readers.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works without them.

Third-party links

Our articles link to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Data retention

We keep emails only as long as needed to respond and for our records, and server logs for a limited period set by our hosting provider.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you by emailing contact@buisinesstoday.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact@buisinesstoday.com.
