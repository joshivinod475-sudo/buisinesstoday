Palm Beach Heads for a Record Year of $30 Million Home Sales, and Most Buyers Are Paying Cash

Palm Beach is on course for a record number of $30 million home sales, mostly paid in cash. Here is what is driving it, and what Indian buyers should know.

Editorial Desk Published 15 Nov 2026, 6:55 pm IST Updated 15 Nov 2026, 7:08 pm IST 5 min read
Share WhatsApp X LinkedIn

In most of the United States, buying a home has become harder this year. Mortgage rates are above 7% and many buyers are priced out. At the very top of the market, the picture is the opposite.

Palm Beach County in Florida is heading for a record number of home sales above $30 million, and this week alone brought a $100 million purchase and a $100 million listing. The reason the top of the market can boom while the rest struggles comes down to one word: cash.

Key points

  • Palm Beach County recorded 21 home sales of $30 million or more in the first nine months of 2026, matching the whole of 2025, according to the Miami Association of Realtors.
  • More than half of the county’s home sales in September were paid for in cash, about double the US average.
  • The median price of a house in the town of Palm Beach was $12 million in the third quarter, brokerage Corcoran reports. Across the wider county it was $656,000.
  • The winter selling season has just begun, with several homes listed at $50 million and above.

A record year at the top

Palm Beach is a narrow island town on Florida’s Atlantic coast, long a winter base for wealthy Americans. Since the pandemic it has also drawn finance firms and their executives from New York, helped by Florida’s lack of a state income tax.

The latest figures show how far that has gone:

  • Sales above $30 million. Palm Beach County had 21 by the end of September. At that pace the Miami Association of Realtors projects 28 for the year, which would be 27% above the previous record. That is a projection, and the final figure could differ.
  • Houses in the town itself. Corcoran counted 29 single-family sales in the third quarter, nearly three times the number a year earlier and the strongest third quarter since 2021. The median price rose 20% to $12 million. Six homes sold for more than $20 million, the highest at $40 million.
  • Supply. Only 70 houses were listed for sale in the town, slightly fewer than a year ago.

“What stands out to me is the depth of demand,” Corcoran chief executive Pamela Liebman said in the firm’s third-quarter report.

One caution on these numbers: they come from estate agents and their trade body, who have an interest in a strong market, and they mostly count resales, not newly built homes.

This week’s deals and listings

South Florida’s selling season runs from November to May, and the most expensive homes tend to come to market now. The property newsletter Buying Power reported these on 13 November:

  • A $100 million purchase. Technology entrepreneur Jim Clark bought a waterfront estate this week. Property deeds recorded a lower figure of $84 million, according to other reports.
  • A $100 million listing. A renovated lakefront estate bought two years ago for $51.6 million is now asking nearly double.
  • A $75 million listing. A 1955 house on 1.4 acres, offered by a family estate and described as likely to be demolished by its buyer. Here the value is the land.
  • A $52.9 million penthouse above the shops of Worth Avenue, bought three years ago for $18 million.

Among recent buyers is Harvey Jones, who bought a lakefront home for $74.3 million two years ago and was named the first chair of Nvidia’s board this week.

Asking prices are not sale prices. A listing at double the purchase price shows a seller’s confidence, and whether buyers agree will only be known when the homes sell. We look at that gap in our report on a Miami Beach estate asking $170 million.

Why high mortgage rates do not slow this market

The explanation is simple. These buyers are not borrowing.

Cash buyers accounted for 51.4% of home sales in Palm Beach County in September, against about 27% nationally, according to figures compiled by the brokerage Manhattan Miami from realtor data. US mortgage rates were about 7.46% in mid-October.

A buyer who pays cash is unaffected by the cost of a loan. What matters to them is the value of their shares, businesses and other assets. That is why the top of the property market tends to follow the stock market, and why it can boom while ordinary buyers are priced out. It is the reverse of how most households experience housing, where the interest rate decides the monthly EMI and so decides what they can afford.

The gap inside one county makes the point. The median house in the town of Palm Beach costs $12 million. The median house in Palm Beach County as a whole costs $656,000, and total sales there fell 3.3% in September from a year earlier.

What this means for Indian readers

The same split exists in India. Property consultancies have reported for the past two years that expensive homes are taking a rising share of sales in India’s big cities, while affordable housing lags. As in Florida, the buyers at the top depend more on wealth than on home loans.

Buying a home abroad has rules. A resident Indian can send up to $250,000 a year overseas under the Reserve Bank of India’s Liberalised Remittance Scheme, and buying property is a permitted use. Tax is collected at source on larger remittances, and overseas property must be declared in the income tax return. The limits and tax rates change, so check the current rules with a bank or tax adviser before sending money.

Property is one asset among several. A single home, in India or abroad, concentrates a lot of money in one place. That is worth weighing against more spread-out options such as index funds, and against trends closer to home such as the changes in commercial property since hybrid work.

What to watch next

  • Year-end figures from Florida’s realtor associations, which will show whether Palm Beach reaches the projected record.
  • US interest rates. The Federal Reserve meets on 9 December. A rise would squeeze ordinary buyers further while leaving cash buyers largely untouched.
  • Florida’s property tax changes, approved by voters on 3 November, which raise the tax exemption for main homes from 2027.
