## Which reMarkable is which?

- **Paper Pro:** the flagship, with a large colour e-ink screen and a front light for reading in the dark.
- **Paper Pro Move:** a smaller, more portable version.
- **Paper Pure:** the lower-priced model, aimed at people who mainly want to write.

All are designed for distraction-free work. There are no social media apps, no email notifications and no video.

## Is an e-ink tablet worth it?

**It suits you if you:**

- take lots of handwritten notes in meetings or lectures
- read and annotate long PDFs, reports or research papers
- want to reduce screen distraction and eye strain
- like the feel of writing on paper but want everything stored digitally

**It probably does not suit you if you:**

- want one device for notes, video, browsing and apps; a regular tablet with a stylus does more for the money
- need fast colour graphics or video
- mainly read e-books, where a cheaper e-reader will do

Also budget for extras. The marker pen and folio are often essential, and reMarkable's **Connect** subscription, which adds features such as unlimited cloud storage and handwriting conversion, costs extra after any trial.

## Tips for Black Friday shopping

1. **Check the "regular" price.** Look at what the product sold for in recent months, not only the crossed-out price.
2. **Compare bundles.** A bundle saves money only if you need every item in it.
3. **Read return policies.** reMarkable has offered a trial period with returns. Check the current terms.
4. **Watch for currency and customs costs** if ordering from another country.
