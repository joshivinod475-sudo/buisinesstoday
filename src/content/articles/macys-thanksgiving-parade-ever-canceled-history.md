This year's Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, the 100th, has been [moved from Thursday to Saturday](/world/macys-thanksgiving-parade-postponed-saturday-storm/) because of a severe storm. It is the first postponement in the parade's history. But it is not the first time the event has been disrupted.

## Key points

- The first Macy's parade was held in **1924**.
- The parade was **cancelled in 1942, 1943 and 1944** during the Second World War.
- In **1971**, strong winds grounded every balloon, but the parade still went ahead.
- In **2020**, the pandemic forced a scaled-down, mostly televised event without public spectators.
- **2026** is the first time the parade has been moved to a different day.

## Why the 2026 parade is the 100th

The parade began in 1924, but because it was not held for three years during the war, the 2026 edition is the 100th parade, not the 103rd.

## The wartime cancellations

After the United States entered the Second World War, Macy's called off the 1942 parade. Rubber and helium were needed for the war effort, including for military blimps. Macy's deflated its balloons and donated the rubber, reportedly about 650 pounds of it, to the government.

The parade stayed off the calendar in 1943 and 1944 and returned in 1945 after the war ended. According to the Macy's Parade Studio and historical accounts, these remain the only years the parade has been cancelled.

## Weather disruptions

**1971:** Wind gusts of up to 40 mph and heavy rain meant no balloons flew for the first time. The parade went ahead without them, and NBC showed footage of balloons from the previous year.

**1997:** High winds caused several balloons to go out of control. A balloon struck a lamppost, injuring spectators, one seriously. The accident led New York to adopt rules limiting balloon flights in strong winds.

**2026:** Organisers moved the parade to Saturday rather than risk a storm on Thursday.

## The pandemic year

In 2020, Covid-19 restrictions led Macy's to hold a pared-down parade concentrated around Herald Square, without public crowds along the route. Balloons were flown using vehicles instead of large teams of handlers, and the traditional public balloon inflation was cancelled. The event was mostly a television production.

## Why the parade matters to Macy's

The parade is one of the world's best-known pieces of brand marketing. It is watched by millions on television and marks the start of the US holiday shopping season, which runs into Black Friday. For retailers, the season can account for a large share of annual sales, a theme in our report on [US holiday hiring](/economy/us-holiday-hiring-2026-seasonal-jobs/).

## Frequently asked questions

**Has the Macy's parade ever been cancelled?**
Yes, in 1942, 1943 and 1944 during the Second World War.

**Has the parade been postponed before 2026?**
No. 2026 is the first postponement.

**When did the balloons not fly?**
In 1971, when high winds grounded every balloon.
