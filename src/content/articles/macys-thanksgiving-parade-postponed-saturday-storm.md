The 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade will not march on Thanksgiving. Macy's, the New York Police Department and city officials announced on Wednesday, 25 November, that the parade would be moved to **Saturday, 28 November, at 8:30am Eastern Time** because of a severe storm forecast for New York on Thursday.

It is the first time in the parade's history that it has been postponed because of the weather.

## Key points

- New date: **Saturday, 28 November, starting at 8:30am ET** (7pm IST).
- The decision was made jointly by Macy's, the NYPD and city officials because of expected heavy rain and strong winds.
- NBC aired last year's parade on Thursday morning in its usual slot.
- The giant balloons, inflated on Wednesday, were deflated that night and taken back to New Jersey.
- Saturday's forecast is drier and calmer, with gusts expected below the limit for flying balloons.

## Why the parade was moved

A powerful storm system brought heavy rain, high winds and coastal flooding to the East Coast over Thanksgiving, disrupting holiday travel. Macy's said it had "made the decision to move the celebration due to expected severe weather", citing safety.

The giant helium balloons are the main concern in high winds. Under New York City rules adopted after a 1997 accident in which a balloon injured spectators, balloons cannot fly if sustained winds exceed 23 miles per hour or gusts exceed 34 miles per hour. Forecasts for Saturday showed gusts of about 20 to 25 mph, CBS New York reported.

## What happened to the balloons

The traditional balloon inflation on the Upper West Side, near the American Museum of Natural History, took place on Wednesday afternoon, drawing crowds who came to see this year's characters take shape. After the postponement was announced, the balloons were deflated and returned to the Macy's Parade Studio in New Jersey to be prepared again for Saturday.

## A first in 100 years

The parade has been cancelled before, during the Second World War, and has gone ahead in terrible weather, including 1971, when winds grounded all the balloons. But it has never been moved to another day. Our explainer on [whether the Macy's parade has ever been cancelled](/world/macys-thanksgiving-parade-ever-canceled-history/) has the full history.

## Thanksgiving travel disruption

The storm also caused hundreds of flight cancellations and delays on Thanksgiving Day, during one of the busiest travel weeks of the year in the US.
