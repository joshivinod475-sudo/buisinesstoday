Early signs point to a stronger holiday hiring season in the United States than last year, but the jobs on offer, and who gets them, are changing.

Seasonal job postings on the jobs site Indeed were running about 10% ahead of last year and 7% above 2019 levels as of September, CNN reported. Seasonal hiring announcements tracked by the outplacement firm Challenger, Gray & Christmas were up 26% through October from last year's record low.

## Key points

- Seasonal postings were about 10% higher than a year earlier in September, according to Indeed.
- Announced seasonal hiring was up 26% through October, according to Challenger, Gray & Christmas, but from a very low base.
- Hiring announcements are concentrated among fewer employers.
- Postings are shifting from shop floors to warehouses and delivery driving.
- Job seekers are taking about six months on average to find work in a "low-hire, low-fire" labour market.

## Where the seasonal jobs are

Holiday jobs used to mean working tills and stacking shelves in shops. The data now shows a broader mix. Growth in Indeed's seasonal postings is coming from warehouse work, picking and packing online orders, and driving delivery vehicles.

That reflects the steady shift of holiday shopping online. A shopper who once queued in a store now orders on a phone, and the work moves to distribution centres and delivery routes.

## Why "up 26%" needs context

Challenger's figure compares this year with last year's record low. A large percentage rise from a small base does not necessarily mean a large number of jobs. Its data also shows announcements concentrated among a smaller number of big employers, so opportunities depend heavily on where those employers operate.

## The wider job market

The holiday season comes during what economists call a "low-hire, low-fire" market. Companies are not laying off many workers, but they are not hiring many either. For people looking for permanent jobs, that means long searches: about six months on average, according to CNN.

For many, a seasonal job is a bridge: income while they keep looking, and sometimes a route to a permanent role. Employers often keep some seasonal staff after the holidays.

The hiring picture fits other data. US [retail sales slowed in October](/economy/us-retail-sales-october-2026-consumers/), with shoppers possibly saving purchases for holiday deals. [Productivity is rising while hours worked stay flat](/economy/us-productivity-q3-2026-ai-boom-workers/), which helps explain why companies are cautious about adding permanent staff.

## Tips for seasonal job seekers

1. **Apply early.** Many employers fill most seasonal roles by mid-November.
2. **Look beyond stores.** Warehouse and delivery roles often pay more per hour and offer extra shifts.
3. **Ask about permanent roles.** Some employers convert seasonal staff after the holidays.
4. **Check the details.** Confirm the hourly rate, shift pattern and whether you are an employee or an independent contractor.

## The Indian parallel

India has its own version during the festive season from Navratri to Diwali, when e-commerce companies and their logistics partners hire large numbers of temporary staff for warehouses and last-mile delivery. The same trend applies: more of the work is in logistics rather than stores, and much of it is contract or gig work without long-term security.
