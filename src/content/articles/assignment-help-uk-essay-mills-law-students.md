Search for "assignment help UK" and you will find hundreds of companies offering to help students with essays, dissertations and coursework. Some offer genuine tutoring. Others will write the work for you, which has been illegal to provide in England since 2022.

For the hundreds of thousands of international students in the UK, many of them from India, the difference matters. Submitting work written by someone else can lead to expulsion and, for students on visas, the loss of their right to stay in the country.

## Key points

- The [Skills and Post-16 Education Act 2022](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/21/part/4/chapter/1) made it a criminal offence to provide or arrange "contract cheating" services for financial gain to students in England, and to advertise them.
- The law targets the companies and writers, not the students. But students still face university penalties.
- Universities treat buying work as serious academic misconduct.
- For international students, misconduct can lead to withdrawal from the course and the end of visa sponsorship.
- Legitimate help, such as tutoring, proofreading within university rules and university skills centres, remains allowed.

## What the law says

The essay mill offence became law on 28 April 2022. It applies when a business or a person working for one, for payment, completes all or part of an assignment for a student, or arranges for someone else to do so, in circumstances where the student is required to complete the work personally.

It covers students:

- at higher education providers in England
- on post-16 courses and in sixth forms in England
- taking regulated qualifications in England as private candidates

**Advertising** such services is also an offence. The [Advertising Standards Authority](https://www.asa.org.uk/news/running-down-those-mills-essay-mills-now-illegal-in-england.html) has advised publishers not to carry essay mill ads. A disclaimer in the company's terms saying the student will not submit the work is not, by itself, a defence.

The law applies in England. Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland have separate education systems, though their universities also treat contract cheating as misconduct.

## What students risk

The law does not prosecute students, but universities have their own rules, and the consequences can be severe.

| Risk | What can happen |
|---|---|
| Academic penalty | A mark of zero, a failed module or a capped grade |
| Disciplinary action | Suspension or expulsion for serious or repeated cases |
| Degree withdrawal | Some universities can revoke a degree if cheating is found later |
| Visa consequences | If a student on a Student visa is withdrawn from their course, the university must report it, and the visa is likely to be cut short |
| Blackmail | Some essay mills threaten to tell the university unless students pay more |

Universities also have better tools for spotting bought work, including checks of writing style, oral examinations and in-person assessments.

## Why the risk is higher for international students

Indian students are one of the largest groups of international students in the UK. They pay high fees, often funded by education loans, and many plan to use the UK's post-study Graduate route to work after graduating.

An expulsion for misconduct can mean:

- losing tuition fees already paid
- having to leave the UK
- having a misconduct finding that can come up in future visa or job applications
- repaying an education loan without the degree it was meant to fund

## Legal help you can use

Not all academic support is cheating. These are generally allowed, within your university's rules:

- **University skills centres.** Most UK universities offer free academic writing, maths and study skills support.
- **Personal tutors and lecturers.** Office hours are there to explain assignments and marking criteria.
- **Library support.** Librarians help with research and referencing.
- **Private tutoring.** A tutor can explain concepts and give feedback, but must not write your work.
- **Proofreading.** Many universities allow limited proofreading for spelling and grammar, under specific policies. Check yours first.

## How to judge an assignment help service

Red flags include:

- offers of "plagiarism-free" or "100% original" essays written for you
- guaranteed grades or "first-class" results
- pressure to pay upfront for an entire dissertation
- no physical address or company registration details

A service that only explains, teaches and gives feedback on work you wrote yourself is very different from one that delivers finished work.

## The business behind it

Contract cheating is a large global industry. Many companies operate from outside the UK and advertise heavily through search engines, social media and sponsored articles on blogs. That marketing is one reason the government targeted advertising in the 2022 law. ## The AI factor

Generative AI tools have changed the picture. Many universities now publish rules on when AI can be used for brainstorming or editing, and when it counts as misconduct. Submitting AI-written work as your own breaks most universities' rules, just like buying an essay. Always check your course's AI policy.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is assignment help illegal in the UK?**
Tutoring and feedback are legal. Providing or advertising a service that completes work for students in England, for payment, has been illegal since April 2022.

**Can a student be prosecuted for buying an essay?**
No. The offence applies to providers and advertisers. Students face university penalties instead.

**Can I lose my UK student visa for cheating?**
If misconduct leads to your withdrawal from the course, your university must report it and your visa is likely to be cut short.

**Is proofreading allowed?**
Often yes, within limits set by your university. Check its proofreading policy.
