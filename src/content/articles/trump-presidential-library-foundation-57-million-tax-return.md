The foundation set up to build Donald Trump's presidential library raised $57.6 million by the end of 2025, according to its tax return, which the foundation provided to CNN.

The filing gives the first official view of the project's finances. It does not name the donors, and it does not show how much of the money came from legal settlements that media and technology companies reached with Trump.

## Key points

- The foundation raised $57.6 million in 2025, more than the roughly $50 million it had told the US tax authority to expect.
- It spent $349,000 in its first seven months, much of it on an architecture firm.
- It has projected raising close to $1 billion by the end of 2027. CNN reported that it is unclear whether that target is still on track.
- None of its named officers or trustees was paid in 2025.

## Where the money has come from

US tax law does not require a foundation of this kind to publish its donors, and this one has not. Some sources are known from court records and company announcements.

| Source | Amount | Background |
|---|---|---|
| ABC News | $15 million | Agreed in December 2024 to settle a defamation claim by Trump |
| Meta | $22 million | Earmarked for the library in a January 2025 settlement |
| Paramount | Part of $16 million | A 2025 settlement covering Trump's legal costs, with the remainder going to the foundation |

The tax return does not confirm how much of these sums the foundation received in 2025.

CNN also reported that two of Trump's senior political advisers, James Blair and Chris LaCivita, recently donated.

## What is planned

The library is designed as a 47-storey tower in downtown Miami. Renderings show a gilded entrance and a display of an Air Force One aircraft, and Trump has suggested the building could include a hotel.

Much of the foundation's early spending went to Bermello Ajamil & Partners, the architecture firm that produced the designs.

The site is 2.63 acres of land transferred in 2025 from Miami Dade College, a public institution, with the help of Florida's governor, Ron DeSantis. The parcel is valued at about $63 million. Several South Florida residents have sued to block the transfer. Trump and the other defendants have asked a federal judge to dismiss the case, which has not been decided.

## Who runs it

The foundation has a five-member board. It includes Trump's son Eric Trump, his son-in-law Michael Boulos and James Kiley, a lawyer who has represented the Trump Organization.

## Why presidential libraries raise questions

Every modern US president has built a library with private money, and the practice has always drawn criticism. Three features make these foundations different from campaign funds.

- **No limit on gifts.** Election campaigns face caps on donations. Library foundations do not.
- **No required disclosure.** Donors can stay anonymous unless the foundation chooses to name them.
- **Fundraising in office.** A sitting president can raise money from companies and individuals who have business before the government.

Senator Elizabeth Warren and other Democrats have proposed legislation to require disclosure and restrict donations while a president is in office. It has not passed.

In this case there is an added element: part of the money comes from companies that settled lawsuits brought by the president while their businesses were subject to decisions by his administration. Critics describe that as a conflict of interest. The companies have presented the settlements as ordinary legal resolutions.

## Why it matters to business readers

For companies, the episode shows how settling a dispute with a head of government can become a reputational and governance issue. Shareholders and regulators may ask why a payment was made and who approved it.

India has faced a version of the same debate. Its electoral bonds scheme, which let companies fund political parties anonymously, was struck down by the Supreme Court in 2024 on the ground that voters have a right to know who finances politics. The underlying question, whether large donors may stay hidden, is the same in both countries.
