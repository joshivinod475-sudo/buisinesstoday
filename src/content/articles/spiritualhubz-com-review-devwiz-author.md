People searching for SpiritualHubz.com often add a second phrase: "USA", "about" or "devwiz author". Each has a simple explanation, and none of them requires taking the site's own description on trust. We looked at the site's pages, its public WordPress data and its domain record.

## Key points

- SpiritualHubz.com is a WordPress blog registered on 5 March 2025. Its first spiritual posts appeared two days later.
- Its About page describes a site about spiritual meanings, dream interpretation and tattoo symbolism.
- Most of its recent posts are on unrelated subjects, such as plastic extrusion, diesel filter cleaning, buying Google reviews and shipping from Amazon to Algeria.
- "Devwiz" is the WordPress username of the account that publishes as "Nathaniel". The site gives no further details about this person.
- Several pages elsewhere describe SpiritualHubz.com in the first person, as if written by the site. They are not the site's own pages.

## Update, 21 November: what "SpiritualHubz.com USA" means

"USA" is now one of the most common words people add when searching for the site. We checked again on 21 November 2026:

- **No US address or company.** The homepage does not mention the USA, the United States or America at all. The only US link is the disclaimer's reference to New York State law.
- **US English settings.** The site's language is set to US English (en-US), its default for American spelling and date formats.
- **No new posts.** The site still has 245 posts. The latest, on Indian plastic extrusion manufacturers, was published on 5 October 2026, so nothing new has appeared for about seven weeks.

In other words, "SpiritualHubz.com USA" describes the site's intended audience, not a US-registered business. Several third-party pages use "USA" in their titles about the site, which is one reason the phrase appears in searches. ## What the site was built for

According to its About page, SpiritualHubz aims to explain "the spiritual significance behind everyday encounters, dreams, and even tattoos". It lists four subjects: spiritual meanings of symbols, numbers and animals; dream interpretation; tattoo meanings; and "mystical insights" on meditation, energy and spiritual awakening.

The early posts match that description. The site's public post list begins with articles such as the meaning of a bird hitting a window, seeing a flock of birds and the spiritual meaning of a roadrunner, published in March 2025. Its homepage still carries the line "Join me as we explore ancient wisdom, sacred teachings, and divine blessings."

The About page says the site has "a team of researchers, writers, and spiritual enthusiasts". It names none of them. The contact details are shown only as a protected email address, and the Contact Us page was blank when we loaded it.

## What it publishes now

The site's public WordPress data showed 245 posts when we checked on 18 November 2026. Publishing peaked in 2025: around 25 posts in March, 36 in July, 35 in October and 24 in November. It slowed sharply in 2026, to one or two posts in most months, with a brief rise to nine in September.

The recent posts have moved far from spiritual subjects. Titles on the homepage when we checked included:

- How Indian plastic extrusion manufacturers support global B2B supply
- Why choose a professional DPF filter cleaning service for your vehicle
- Is buying Google reviews a good strategy for building trust?
- Shopping at Amazon and having it delivered to Algeria
- Window glass tinting
- A guide to another website, Replixi.com

The site's categories tell the same story. Besides a general "Blog" category with 133 posts and "Others" with 68, there are categories for automobiles, business, CBD, crypto, fashion, law, home improvement and finance.

A spiritual blog publishing articles on industrial plastics and car exhaust filters is a pattern seen across many small websites. These sites often publish articles supplied by outside businesses, frequently with a link back to the business, sometimes for a fee. We cannot say that is what happens on SpiritualHubz.com, because the posts we saw carry no label either way. ## Who is "devwiz"?

"Devwiz" is a WordPress username, also called a slug. The site's public user list shows one account with the slug "devwiz" and the display name "Nathaniel". The account has no biography, no website link and no profile beyond a default avatar. Recent posts on all subjects, from therapy to plastic extrusion, appear under this name.

That is all that can be confirmed. We found no evidence linking the username to a named company or person outside the site, and we would treat any page that claims to know more without a source with caution. A single account posting on many unrelated subjects usually means the account is used to upload content, not that one person wrote everything.

## Pages that speak for the site

Search for "SpiritualHubz.com about" and some results come from other websites that describe the blog in the first person: "our mission", "we write about", "you are in the right place". These pages are not run by SpiritualHubz.com. Their descriptions also differ from the site's real About page. One presents it as a general mindfulness and personal growth blog and does not mention the plastics, car and marketing posts.

When many websites publish near-identical articles about a small site, often written to capture searches for its name, the search term begins to look more important than it is. ## Is it safe to use?

We saw nothing on the pages we visited asking for payment or personal details beyond a standard comment form. As with any site, apply normal care:

- **Spiritual and dream content is belief, not fact.** The site's own disclaimer says its interpretations "are not absolute truths and should not be relied upon as definitive guidance".
- **Health articles are not medical advice.** The site's earliest post was about helping someone having a stroke. In an emergency, call your local emergency number immediately. In the US that is 911, in India 112.
- **Check advice on money or services elsewhere.** A sponsored-style article about a service is not an independent review. Before trusting financial or business tips, see our guidance on [spotting online scams](/tech/facestealer-fake-video-call-scam-fbi-warning/).

## How to judge any website in two minutes

1. **Read the About page.** Does it name real people or a company?
2. **Compare it with the latest posts.** Do they match what the site says it covers?
3. **Look at the author page.** Does the author have a biography and a consistent subject?
4. **Check the domain's age.** Free lookup tools show when a domain was registered.
5. **Look for disclosure.** Reputable sites label sponsored or paid content.
