Indiana's defence of its national title is in danger. The Hoosiers fell to **No. 14** in the College Football Playoff committee's rankings released on Tuesday, 24 November, after a 27–24 overtime loss at Washington. With one regular-season game left, Curt Cignetti's team no longer controls its own path to the Big Ten championship game.

## Key points

- Indiana is **9–2 overall and 6–2 in the Big Ten**.
- Washington won in overtime after a game-tying Indiana field goal attempt went wide.
- At No. 14, Indiana is outside the projected 12-team field.
- The Hoosiers must **beat Purdue** in their final regular-season game and need help from other results to reach the **Big Ten title game on 5 December** in Indianapolis.
- A Big Ten title would give Indiana an automatic playoff place as a conference champion. Without it, an at-large bid looks difficult from No. 14.

## How Indiana got here

The Hoosiers went to Seattle with a playoff place in sight. In overtime, Indiana's 37-yard field goal attempt to tie the game went wide, handing Washington a 27–24 win, as [ESPN reported](https://www.espn.com/college-football/story/_/id/50556327/indiana-cfp-hopes-take-hit-ot-loss-washington).

The loss dropped Indiana several places in the rankings. Analysts have also pointed to Indiana's relatively weak strength of schedule as a factor that hurts it in comparison with other teams with two losses.

## The Big Ten picture

| Team | Big Ten record |
|---|---|
| UCLA | 7–1 |
| Oregon | 6–2 |
| Iowa | 6–2 |
| Indiana | 6–2 |

UCLA leads after its first conference loss, an 18–16 triple-overtime defeat at Michigan. The top two teams in the standings meet in the title game, and tiebreakers will decide places among teams with the same record.

## What Indiana needs

**Step one: beat Purdue.** A loss in the annual Old Oaken Bucket game would end Indiana's Big Ten title hopes.

**Step two: get help.** Indiana needs some combination of losses from UCLA, Oregon and Iowa to finish in the top two. Different outlets have set out slightly different scenarios, because multi-team tiebreakers are complicated, so the exact path depends on results across the league on Saturday.

**Step three: win the Big Ten title.** As conference champion, Indiana would be guaranteed a playoff place.

## Could Indiana get an at-large bid?

At No. 14 with two losses, Indiana would need several teams above it to lose this weekend and in conference championship games. Projections published before Tuesday's rankings gave Indiana roughly a one-in-three to one-in-two chance of making the playoff, but those estimates were made before its fall in the rankings and are likely to have dropped.

## Cignetti's turnaround

Cignetti took over in late 2023 and transformed one of college football's least successful programmes. Indiana reached the playoff in his first season and won the national championship last season. This season's two losses have tested that run, but a win over Purdue and the right results elsewhere would still put the Hoosiers back in contention.
