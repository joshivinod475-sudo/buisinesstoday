A train journey through Eastern Canada connects some of the country's most historic cities with the Atlantic coast. Most trips combine two services run by the national operator, VIA Rail: the busy **Corridor** between Toronto, Ottawa, Montreal and Quebec City, and the overnight **Ocean** between Montreal and Halifax.

Tour operators package these routes with hotels and excursions. Whether you book a tour or plan independently, here is what is worth knowing before you go.

## Key points

- The **Ocean** runs between Montreal and Halifax in roughly a day, usually overnight, and is one of North America's oldest named passenger trains.
- The **Corridor** trains are fast daytime services linking the main cities of Ontario and Quebec.
- Sleeper classes turn a night of travel into a night of accommodation.
- Autumn foliage season, from late September to mid-October, is the most popular and expensive time to travel.
- Indian passport holders need a Canadian visitor visa.

## The main routes

| Route | Journey | What to expect |
|---|---|---|
| Toronto–Ottawa–Montreal–Quebec City (Corridor) | A few hours between each city | Frequent daytime trains, good for city-hopping |
| Montreal–Halifax (Ocean) | About a day, overnight | Sleeper cabins, dining car, scenery along the St Lawrence and through New Brunswick |

The Ocean does not run every day, so check the timetable before planning. Many travellers break the trip with excursions in the Maritime provinces.

## Sleeping on the train

On the Ocean, you can choose a seat or a sleeper. Sleeper options range from berths to private cabins with their own washroom. Because the train travels overnight, a sleeper replaces a hotel night and you wake up in a different province.

Seats are cheaper but make for a long night. If you travel in a seat, bring a neck pillow, an eye mask and warm clothes.

## Highlights along the way

- **Quebec City:** a walled old town that is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
- **Montreal:** a bilingual city with a strong food culture.
- **Halifax:** a port city and gateway to Nova Scotia.
- **Peggy's Cove and Lunenburg:** a famous lighthouse and a UNESCO-listed colonial town near Halifax.
- **Bay of Fundy:** home to the highest tides in the world, best seen at the Hopewell Rocks.
- **Prince Edward Island:** beaches, farms and seafood.

## When to go

| Season | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Summer (June–August) | Long days, festivals, whale watching | Busy and expensive |
| Autumn (late September–October) | Spectacular foliage | Highest demand; book early |
| Spring and late autumn | Fewer crowds, lower prices | Cooler weather; some attractions close |
| Winter | Snowy scenery, winter festivals | Cold, shorter days, possible delays |

## Comparing tour prices

Packaged rail tours vary widely in length, hotels and included excursions. A useful way to compare them is the **cost per day**: longer tours are not always more expensive per day, because fixed costs are spread over more nights. Check exactly what is included, such as meals, transfers and entry fees.

## Practical tips for Indian travellers

- **Visa:** Indian citizens need a Canadian visitor visa, which requires biometrics. Apply well in advance, as processing times can be long. Check the official [Government of Canada visa website](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada.html).
- **Money:** use a forex card or a credit card with low foreign transaction fees. Under India's Liberalised Remittance Scheme, tax is collected at source on overseas tour packages above set thresholds; check the current rules when booking.
- **Food:** major cities have plenty of vegetarian and Indian options. On the train, inform VIA Rail of dietary needs in advance.
- **Insurance:** buy travel insurance that covers medical costs, which are high in Canada for visitors.

## Frequently asked questions

**How long is the train from Montreal to Halifax?**
About a day. VIA Rail's Ocean usually travels overnight.

**Is there a sleeper train in Eastern Canada?**
Yes. The Ocean offers berths and private cabins.

**What is the best time for an Eastern Canada train tour?**
Late September to mid-October for autumn colours, or summer for long days and festivals.

**Do Indians need a visa for Canada?**
Yes. Indian passport holders need a visitor visa.
