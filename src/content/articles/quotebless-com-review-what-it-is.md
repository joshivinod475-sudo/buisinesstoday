Quotebless.com is a website of ready-made quotes, blessings, wishes and text messages. Its menu has five sections: Blessings, Quotes, Responses, Paragraphs and Wishes. A typical post is a long list, such as "250+ Good Night Blessings" or "210+ Short Funny Friday Quotes", written for readers to copy and send.

People searching for it often add "USA". The site writes in American English, and its seasonal posts (Fall quotes, Sunday Funday captions, US-style holidays) are aimed at an American audience. We checked its pages, its public WordPress data and its domain record to see what else it is.

## Key points

- **What it is:** a WordPress site with 178 published posts as of 21 November 2026.
- **Domain age:** quotebless.com was registered on 17 August 2025, so the site is about 15 months old.
- **Who runs it:** the sidebar says "I'm Umer, the heart behind QuoteBless". On the site's WordPress data, 176 of the 178 posts are published under an account named "Dilawar Mughal".
- **Content mix:** most posts are quotes and messages, but recent ones include cosmetic surgery, baby formula, PVC shutters, plastic enclosures and a court procedure in Kansas City.
- **Safety:** copying a birthday message carries no risk. Treat its health, legal and product posts as unverified.

## What quotebless.com publishes

The site's own sections match its name. When we checked, the homepage listed posts such as:

- **Quotes:** Daughters Day quotes from a mother, autumn quotes, funny Saturday and Sunday quotes
- **Blessings:** Wednesday blessings, October blessings, birthday, good night and good afternoon blessings
- **Paragraphs:** long paragraphs for a best friend, a boyfriend or a crush
- **Responses:** what a white heart emoji means, how to reply to "I'm sorry", how to deal with dry texts

The category counts tell a broader story. The largest category is "Others" with 124 posts, followed by "News" (33), "Responses" (23), "Blessings" (22), "Tech News" (16) and "Quotes" (13). There are also categories for business, health, law, real estate, CBD and home improvement, and one spelled "Beauti".

## The posts that do not fit

The site's newest posts, published from September to November 2026, are mostly about subjects unrelated to quotes:

- what makes a PVC rolling shutter profile manufacturer suitable for OEM supply

A quotes website carrying posts about factory components and surgery is a familiar pattern. Small sites with steady search traffic often publish articles supplied by outside businesses, usually with a link back to that business, and sometimes for a fee. The posts on quotebless.com carry no "sponsored" label, so we cannot say whether any were paid for. ## Who runs it

The About page says many quotes are "written by our team, while others are carefully selected from timeless and inspiring sources". It does not name the team. The sidebar introduces the site's founder only as "Umer", who enjoys "stargazing, learning new skills, and spending time with loved ones".

The site's WordPress user list shows three accounts: "Dilawar Mughal", with 176 posts, and two others with one post each. The site does not explain how "Umer" and "Dilawar Mughal" are related. The Contact page offers a form and a protected email address and says replies take three to five days. The footer links to editorial guidelines, a privacy policy, terms and a disclaimer.

The domain is registered through Namecheap with the owner's details hidden, which is normal. The site is served through Cloudflare.

## Is quotebless.com safe?

**For quotes and messages:** yes, in the sense that matters. Nothing on the pages we visited asked for payment or personal details beyond a contact form.

**Credit for quotes:** the site says some quotes come from other sources. Before using a quote in a speech, presentation or company post, check who actually said it. Misattributed quotes spread easily online.

**Health and legal posts:** be careful. Choose baby formula with a paediatrician's advice. Get information about surgery from a qualified surgeon. Get information about court procedures from a lawyer or the court itself. Our reviews of [SpiritualHubz.com](/marketing/spiritualhubz-com-review-devwiz-author/) and [Tozymagazine.com](/marketing/tozymagazine-com-review-what-it-is/) found the same mix of lifestyle posts and commercial articles.

## Why sites like this matter to businesses

For a business, a quotes site looks like an easy place to buy a link. That carries risk. Google's spam policies treat links bought to manipulate rankings as link spam, and a link on an unrelated quotes page gives readers little reason to click. A relevant mention on a site in your own industry is worth more, both to readers and to search engines.
