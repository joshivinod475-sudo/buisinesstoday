You can read a great deal of manga online for free without using pirate sites. Japan's biggest publishers now release many chapters free on their own apps, often on the same day they appear in Japan, and pay for it with ads, subscriptions and coin purchases.

The catch is that each service covers only its own publisher's titles, and "free" usually means some chapters, not whole back catalogues. This guide sets out what the main legal options offer, which well-known services have closed, and why the unofficial sites are a bigger risk than they look.

## Key points

- **MANGA Plus**, run by the publisher Shueisha, is the biggest free legal source, with new chapters of series such as One Piece on release day.
- **Webtoon** is free to read and focuses on vertical-scroll webcomics, including many Korean titles, rather than Japanese manga.
- Publisher apps such as **K MANGA** (Kodansha) and the **Shonen Jump** app (VIZ Media) mix free chapters with paid access.
- Library apps can lend complete manga volumes free with a library card where the service is available.
- Several names still found in "best manga site" lists, such as Crunchyroll Manga and the standalone ComiXology app, have closed.
- Bato.to, one of the largest pirate manga networks, went offline in January 2026 after a criminal case in China.

### MANGA Plus

Shueisha publishes Weekly Shonen Jump, Japan's best-selling manga magazine. Its MANGA Plus app and website publish official English translations, along with Spanish, Thai, Indonesian and other languages, for free. You can usually read the first few and the most recent few chapters of each series, which suits readers who are up to date. A paid tier unlocks the full back catalogue in some regions. MANGA Plus is available in India.

### Shonen Jump app and VIZ

VIZ Media, part-owned by Shueisha, publishes Shueisha's titles in North America. Its Shonen Jump app gives the newest chapters free and opens a large vault of older chapters for a monthly fee of a few dollars. It is the main legal route for US readers who want to catch up on a long series.

### K MANGA

Kodansha's app publishes series from the company's magazines. Readers get a limited number of free chapters, refreshed over time, and can buy points to read more. Availability depends on your country.

### Webtoon

Webtoon is not strictly a manga service. It publishes webcomics designed for scrolling on a phone, many of them Korean (manhwa) or by independent creators. Most series are free with ads, while the newest episodes can be unlocked early with coins.

### Library apps

In countries where public libraries support them, such as the US, apps like Libby and Hoopla lend complete manga volumes, free, with a library card. This is often the best legal way to read a finished series from start to end without paying.

## Services that have closed

Many lists of "best manga websites" are out of date. Before you go looking, note:

- **Crunchyroll Manga** shut down at the end of 2023. Crunchyroll still streams anime but no longer offers a manga reader.
- **ComiXology**, the digital comics store, was merged into Amazon's Kindle store in 2023. Its titles are now bought and read through Kindle.

Any guide that still recommends these is likely to be copying older lists without checking.

## Why pirate manga sites are risky

Unofficial "scanlation" sites post fan-translated pages without the publisher's permission. They often carry every chapter, which is why they rank well in searches. There are good reasons to avoid them.

**They are being shut down.** Bato.to and dozens of linked sites went offline in January 2026 after Chinese authorities detained the operator, following a complaint by Japanese publishers. Japan's Content Overseas Distribution Association (CODA) said the network recorded about 350 million visits in a single month in 2025, and estimated lost sales at about 770 billion yen, around $5 billion. That figure is an industry estimate. Readers lose their place, bookmarks and reading history when a site disappears.

**They are a common route for malware.** Pirate sites pay their costs with aggressive advertising. Fake "download" and "next chapter" buttons, pop-ups that ask you to allow notifications, and redirects to fake prize or app pages are common. Our reports on [fake job offers that install malware](/tech/trigram-fake-job-offers-developers-north-korea-malware/) and the [FaceStealer fake video call scam](/tech/facestealer-fake-video-call-scam-fbi-warning/) show how ordinary-looking clicks lead to stolen data.

**Creators are not paid.** Manga artists are paid through sales and readership figures. Pirate reading does not count, which can weigh on the series readers want to continue.

### Safety checklist for any manga site

1. Check who runs it. Official services name the publisher.
2. Never install an app from outside the Google Play Store or Apple's App Store to "read for free".
3. Refuse browser notification requests from reading sites.
4. Do not enter phone numbers or card details for "free" prizes or "verification".
5. Use an ad blocker and keep your browser up to date.

## Why publishers now give manga away

The free chapters are a business decision. Publishers learned that pirate sites were meeting demand for fast, cheap translations that the official industry had ignored. Releasing chapters free on release day reduces the reason to use pirate sites, builds a global readership, and feeds sales of print volumes, anime licences and merchandise.

The model resembles other freemium apps: give away the newest content, charge for the back catalogue or for convenience, and sell ads in between. The same thinking shapes much of the software industry we cover in our [technology section](/category/tech/).

## Frequently asked questions

**Is MANGA Plus really free?**
Yes, for a selection of chapters of each series, including the latest. A paid plan unlocks more in some regions.

**Where can I read One Piece legally for free?**
On MANGA Plus, which publishes new chapters on release day. VIZ's Shonen Jump app also carries it, with the back catalogue behind its paid vault.

**What is the best free manga app for Android and iPhone?**
For Japanese manga, MANGA Plus has the strongest free selection. For webcomics and manhwa, Webtoon. Both are in the official app stores.

**Is it illegal to read manga on pirate sites?**
Pirate sites break copyright law by publishing without permission. The legal position of readers differs from country to country, but the security risks are the same everywhere.
