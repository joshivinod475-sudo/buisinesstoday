Nvidia shares fell about 6% on Tuesday, giving back part of a huge rally the day before, when the company announced that Jensen Huang would return as chief executive. On Monday the stock closed more than 30% higher, recovering nearly all the losses since Huang disclosed his affair, and adding nearly $1 trillion in market value.

Other chip stocks followed the same pattern. After strong gains on Monday, they opened lower on Tuesday.

## Key points

- **Monday:** Nvidia closed up about 31% after Huang's reinstatement.
- **Tuesday:** shares fell about 6% in morning trading.
- **Other chipmakers on Tuesday:** AMD down nearly 9%, Micron down 8%, Marvell down 5%, Arm down more than 5%.
- **Analysts:** several upgraded Nvidia after the news. Mizuho said the return "RETURNS Investor CONFIDENCE in the AI trade".
- **Risks:** investigations, congressional hearings and export-control questions remain.

## Why the stock swung so much

Investors see Huang as central to Nvidia. He co-founded the company in 1993 and has run it ever since, steering it from graphics cards to AI computing. His resignation removed the person most investors associate with its strategy and its relationships with customers such as Microsoft, Meta and OpenAI.

When he left, Nvidia's shares fell sharply, which helped drag the Nasdaq to [its worst week since 2020](/markets/anthropic-ipo-debut-nasdaq-worst-week-venture-capital/). His return reversed that move almost in one day.

Tuesday's fall reflects normal profit-taking after a record one-day gain, as well as a reminder that the underlying issues have not gone away.

## What could still move the stock

| Factor | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Board investigation | A finding that confidential information was shared could reopen the leadership question |
| Congressional hearings | Huang faces House committees on 1 December and the Senate Banking Committee on 3 December |
| California and SEC inquiries | Possible penalties or restatements |
| Export controls | Tighter rules on chip sales to China would hit revenue |
| Interest rates | The Fed is expected to [raise rates in December](/markets/treasury-yields-fed-rate-hike-december-ai-inflation/), which weighs on growth stocks |

Senator Elizabeth Warren, who becomes chair of the Senate Banking Committee in January, called the reinstatement "reckless" and said Nvidia still owes Congress answers on export controls.

## The bigger picture

Nvidia had already reported [record quarterly revenue and raised its forecast](/markets/nvidia-q3-earnings-record-revenue-after-huang/) while Huang was absent, which showed that demand for its chips remained strong. The past two weeks show how much the market's view of a company can depend on one person, a risk known as "key person risk".

For Indian investors who own Nvidia through US stocks or international funds, the episode is a reminder that single-stock exposure can swing by double-digit percentages in a day.
