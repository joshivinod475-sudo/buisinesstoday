18K gold is 75% pure gold mixed with 25% other metals such as copper, silver, zinc or palladium. It is harder than 22K gold, which makes it the usual choice for diamond and gemstone jewellery, lightweight daily-wear pieces and white or rose gold designs.

For Indian buyers, the important questions are practical. How does the price compare with 22K? How do you check purity? And how much will you get back when you sell? This guide answers each one.

## Key points

- **Purity:** 18K = 18 parts gold out of 24 = 75% gold, stamped "18K750" on hallmarked jewellery in India.
- **Price:** the gold value of 18K jewellery is roughly 75% of the 24K rate, before making charges and GST.
- **Hallmark:** BIS hallmarking with a six-digit HUID code is mandatory for gold jewellery sold in India.
- **Strength:** 18K is more scratch-resistant than 22K, so it holds stones more securely.
- **Resale:** you are paid for the gold content only. Making charges and most of the stone value are usually lost.

## 18K vs 22K vs 24K gold

| Purity | Gold content | Hallmark fineness | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 24K | 99.9% | 999 | Coins and bars, rarely jewellery because it is too soft |
| 22K | 91.6% | 916 | Traditional Indian jewellery: necklaces, bangles, chains |
| 18K | 75% | 750 | Diamond and gemstone jewellery, lightweight and western designs |
| 14K | 58.5% | 585 | Budget and fashion jewellery |

The lower the purity, the harder the metal and the paler the colour. 22K has the deep yellow colour most Indian families associate with gold. 18K is slightly lighter in tone and much more durable.

## Yellow, white and rose gold

The 25% of non-gold metal decides the colour:

- **Yellow gold:** gold mixed mainly with silver and copper.
- **Rose gold:** a higher share of copper gives a pinkish tone.
- **White gold:** gold mixed with palladium or nickel, usually plated with rhodium for a bright white finish. The plating wears off over the years and may need redoing.

All three can be 18K. The purity is the same, only the alloy changes.

## How 18K jewellery is priced in India

Jewellers usually price gold jewellery in four parts:

1. **Gold value** = weight in grams × the day's 18K rate
2. **Making charges**, either a fixed amount per gram or a percentage of the gold value, often 8% to 25% for 18K designs
3. **Stone value**, for diamonds or gems, charged separately
4. **GST** at 3% on the value of the jewellery including making charges

**Example:** take a 10-gram 18K chain, with the 24K rate at ₹X per gram.

- 18K rate ≈ 0.75 × X
- Gold value = 10 × 0.75X = 7.5X
- Making charges at 12% = 0.9X
- Subtotal = 8.4X
- GST at 3% = 0.252X
- **Total ≈ 8.65X**

Daily reference rates for different purities are published by the [India Bullion and Jewellers Association](https://ibjarates.com/), and most jewellers display their own 18K and 22K rates. Ask for an itemised bill showing net gold weight, purity, rate, making charges, stone weight and GST.

## How to check purity: the BIS hallmark

Since 2021, hallmarking has been mandatory for gold jewellery sold by registered jewellers in most of India. Since 1 April 2023, every hallmarked piece must carry a six-digit alphanumeric **HUID** (Hallmark Unique Identification) code. A valid hallmark has three marks:

1. The **BIS logo**
2. The **purity grade**, for example "18K750"
3. The **six-digit HUID**

You can check any HUID using the Bureau of Indian Standards' free **BIS CARE** app. It shows the purity, the jeweller's registration and the hallmarking centre. If the details do not match the item, do not buy it. More information is on the [Bureau of Indian Standards](https://www.bis.gov.in/) website.

## Resale and exchange: what you get back

When you sell or exchange 18K jewellery:

- The jeweller tests the purity and weighs the gold, excluding stones.
- You are paid for the gold content at the 18K rate, often minus a deduction of a few per cent.
- **Making charges are not refunded.**
- **Diamonds and stones** are usually bought back at a fraction of their price, or not at all, unless the jeweller has a buyback policy for its own stones.

This is why 18K diamond jewellery is a poor investment, even when it makes a good gift. ## Is 18K gold a good choice?

**Choose 18K if:**

- you want diamond or gemstone jewellery
- you need daily-wear pieces that resist scratches and bending
- you prefer white or rose gold

**Choose 22K if:**

- you want traditional jewellery with a deep yellow colour
- you care about resale value
- the piece is plain gold without stones

## Taxes on selling gold jewellery

Gains on selling physical gold, including jewellery, are taxed as capital gains. Since the 2024 Budget, gold held for more than 24 months is taxed as a long-term capital gain at 12.5% without indexation. Gains on gold held for a shorter period are added to your income and taxed at your slab rate. Exchanging old jewellery for new is generally still treated as a sale for tax purposes. Consult a tax adviser for your own situation.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is 18K gold real gold?**
Yes. It is 75% pure gold. The rest is other metals added for strength and colour.

**Does 18K gold tarnish?**
It does not rust, but the other metals in it can react slightly over many years. White gold's rhodium plating can wear off. Regular cleaning keeps it bright.

**Is 18K gold hallmarking mandatory in India?**
Yes. 18K is one of the standard purity grades covered by mandatory BIS hallmarking with a HUID code.

**Why is 18K gold cheaper than 22K?**
It contains less gold: 75% compared with 91.6%. Making charges for intricate 18K designs can narrow the difference.
