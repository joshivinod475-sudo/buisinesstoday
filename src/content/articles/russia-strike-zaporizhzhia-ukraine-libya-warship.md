A Russian overnight attack on the southern Ukrainian city of Zaporizhzhia killed two civilians and wounded 48 others, including four children, officials said on Tuesday. At the same time, Ukraine and Libya argued over Kyiv's unprecedented drone strike on a Russian warship docked in the Libyan port of Benghazi.

The Zaporizhzhia glide bomb attack destroyed a private house and partly destroyed a five-storey apartment block, said regional military administration head Mykhailo Semikin. The youngest child wounded was less than a year old.

## Key points

- **Zaporizhzhia attack:** 2 killed and 48 wounded, including children aged under 1, 4, 6 and 16.
- **Reinforcements:** President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Ukraine is sending troops to the Zaporizhzhia region, which lies on the roughly 1,250-km front line.
- **Libya strike:** Ukraine struck a Russian warship in Benghazi with naval drones on Sunday. Libya's Tripoli-based Foreign Ministry said it had not authorised any foreign military operation.
- **Casualties:** the UN says nearly 18,000 Ukrainian civilians have been killed since February 2022.
- **New estimate:** independent Russian outlets Meduza and Mediazona estimate more than 700,000 Russian soldiers have died, based partly on hacked government data. The AP could not verify the figure.

## Escalation during peace talks

Russia and Ukraine have stepped up air strikes in recent months even as they take part in US-mediated talks to end the war. Russia is targeting Ukraine's power system before winter. Ukraine has answered with long-range strikes, hitting targets as far away as Siberia, the Crimean Bridge and Russian ships.

The strike in Libya was the farthest yet. Ukraine said it meant no harm to Libya. Libya is split between rival authorities: the internationally recognised government in Tripoli and forces in the east, where Benghazi lies, which have close ties to Russia.

## How many soldiers have died?

| Source | Estimate of Russian military deaths |
|---|---|
| CSIS think tank (January 2026) | Up to 325,000, February 2022–December 2025 |
| UK Chief of the Defence Staff (two months ago) | More than 500,000 |
| Meduza and Mediazona (November 2026) | More than 700,000 |

Meduza and Mediazona said their new figure used a large dataset hacked from Russia's statistics agency, Rosstat, by Belarusian activists and a Ukrainian hacker group, cross-checked against public records. Russia has not published official battlefield death figures for several years. All such estimates should be treated with caution.

## Why it matters for the world economy

- **Energy:** attacks on Russian energy infrastructure and shipping affect oil and gas markets, already disrupted by the Iran war.
- **Shipping:** strikes in the Black Sea, the Baltic and now the Mediterranean raise insurance costs for vessels.
- **Wider tensions:** Russia has accused NATO member Estonia of hostile actions, as we report in our update on [Estonia's border closure](/world/estonia-close-russia-border-crossings/).

For India, which buys Russian oil and has called for dialogue, a longer and wider conflict means continued volatility in energy prices and shipping costs.
