Our editorial standards

  • Accuracy first. We check facts against primary sources such as regulators, company filings and official statistics, and link to them wherever we can.
  • Independence. Sponsored or partner content is always clearly labelled. Advertisers do not influence our editorial coverage.
  • Corrections. If we get something wrong, we fix it and note the correction on the article. Spotted an error? Email contact@buisinesstoday.com.
  • No advice. We explain; we do not tell you what to buy or sell. Please read our disclaimer.
