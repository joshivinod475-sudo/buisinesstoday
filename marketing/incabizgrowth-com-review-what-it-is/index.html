IncaBizGrowth.com: What the Business Blog Is, Its Domain History and What We Checked

IncaBizGrowth.com is a business, marketing and finance blog with 111 posts and free calculators. The domain dates from 2017 and has changed shape several times. Here is what we found.

Editorial Desk Published 23 Nov 2026, 9:08 pm IST 4 min read
Share WhatsApp X LinkedIn

IncaBizGrowth.com, which styles itself “Inca Biz Growth”, is a blog about business growth. It publishes articles on startups, marketing, SEO, finance, technology and leadership, and offers a set of free business calculators. Its tagline promises “actionable insights on startups, marketing, finance, and leadership”.

The name sounds like a software tool or consultancy, but the site is a content publication. We checked its pages, its WordPress data, its domain record and its history in the Internet Archive on 23 November 2026.

Key points

  • Size: 111 published posts. The oldest surviving posts date from 2021.
  • Domain age: registered on 18 April 2017, making it older than most similar blogs.
  • Authors: most posts are credited to an account called “itsdevin” (77 posts), followed by “Shivi Hyde” (19).
  • Policies: the site has About, Contact, contributor guidelines, advertising, editorial policy and disclaimer pages.
  • History: the domain has gone through at least three designs and content phases since 2017.

What it publishes

The main sections are Business, Finance, Technology, Skills & Education, Health & Wellbeing and SEO. Recent posts include:

  • an explainer on the Crystal Crop Protection IPO and how to check allotment status
  • PPC versus SEO for small businesses
  • targeting keywords with zero search volume
  • LinkedIn brand awareness for B2B companies
  • how to sell on Amazon in 2026
  • a Bahrain court ruling on claims against HDFC Bank over Credit Suisse AT1 bonds

The site also hosts posts on unrelated subjects, from Princess Diana’s “revenge dress” to moving services and appearance tips, in categories such as Health (23 posts) and a general “Blog” category (20).

A domain with several lives

Internet Archive snapshots show how the site has changed:

Year What the homepage showed
2017 “Inc A Biz Growth”, a default WordPress site with a business theme and placeholder text
2019 “Inc A Biz Growth – Multi-Topic Blog”, with posts on scrap metal recycling, cake recipes and car problems
2022 “Inca Biz Growth” with business, education, health and technology categories
2026 The current design, focused on business growth, with calculators and editorial pages

The posts from 2019 are no longer on the site. The surviving archive starts in 2021, so the content was either removed or rebuilt at some point. Older domains with existing links are valuable in search, which is one reason such sites are relaunched rather than replaced.

Who runs it

The About page describes “a team of business writers, marketers, and industry researchers” but does not name an owner or company. The Contact page offers a form with topics including corrections, partnerships and advertising, and expert reviews.

The site’s contributor guidelines page says it accepts original business articles and rules out “casino, CBD, adult, or link-placement articles”. Its footer also links to an advertising page. Readers should be aware that some posts may come from outside contributors or advertisers, as with many blogs of this kind.

Is IncaBizGrowth.com safe?

To read: yes. We saw no harmful downloads or requests for payment.

Calculators: free business calculators can be useful for rough estimates. Check the assumptions behind any result before making decisions.

Financial posts: the IPO and banking articles are general explainers. For investment decisions, rely on official sources such as company filings, SEBI and the stock exchanges, not blog summaries.

What small business owners can learn

The site’s own topics, such as SEO, content marketing and LinkedIn strategy, reflect what many small businesses are trying to do online. Three lessons apply to any business blog:

  1. Consistency beats volume. A steady flow of useful posts on a clear subject builds more trust than bursts of unrelated content.
  2. Name your authors. Readers and search engines both value clear authorship.
  3. Avoid paid-link schemes. Buying links on unrelated blogs can bring penalties. Our guide to Google’s link spam rules explains why.

Frequently asked questions

What is IncaBizGrowth.com? A business blog covering startups, marketing, SEO, finance and technology, with free calculators.

Is IncaBizGrowth.com a business growth tool? No. Despite the name, it is a content website, although it offers some free calculators.

How old is IncaBizGrowth.com? The domain was registered in April 2017. The current content dates from 2021 onwards.

Does IncaBizGrowth.com accept contributed articles? Its contributor guidelines page invites original business article pitches through its contact form and says it does not accept link-placement articles.
