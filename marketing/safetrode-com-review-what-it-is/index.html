Safetrode.com: What the Site Is, Who Runs It and Whether It Is Safe to Read

Safetrode.com was once an earthing company's website in Chennai. It is now a general blog with 83 posts on tech, finance and law. Here is what its pages and domain record show.

Editorial Desk Published 21 Nov 2026, 12:01 am IST 5 min read
Share WhatsApp X LinkedIn

Safetrode.com is a general-interest blog. It calls itself “Safe Trode” and publishes articles on technology, finance, business, law, education, health and home improvement. It is not a trading platform, a broker or an online shop, and it sells nothing directly to readers.

The name causes confusion because it once belonged to a completely different business. We checked the site’s pages, its public WordPress data, its domain registration and old snapshots in the Internet Archive to work out what it is today.

Key points

  • What it is: a WordPress blog with 83 published posts as of 21 November 2026.
  • Who writes it: every post is published under a single account named “Kevin”. The site does not give a full name, a company name or a postal address.
  • Domain history: safetrode.com was registered in February 2019. In 2021 it was the website of a chemical earthing company in Chennai.
  • Content mix: Technology (27 posts) and Finance (16) are the largest sections. Several posts promote specific companies, online casinos or crypto purchases.
  • Safety: reading the site is low-risk, but treat its financial, gambling and product claims as unverified marketing.

Recent titles show the range. They include advice on hiring Java developers, a guide to apostilles in South Carolina, a piece on why investors are funding humanoid robots, an explainer on keeping photos of electrical work, a post on copying futures trades across several accounts, and an article on liability insurance for contractors.

The oldest post still online dates from January 2025. The newest was published on 29 October 2026. Publishing is irregular: three posts went up on a single day in August 2026, followed by gaps of several weeks.

Who runs it

The site’s About page describes it as a publication with “expert articles” and a commitment to “trustworthy content”. It does not name an editor, a company or a country. The Contact page gives only an email address, and lists “advertising requests” and “partnership opportunities” among the enquiries it handles.

Every post is attributed to one WordPress user, “Kevin”. No author biography or credentials appear. That matters most for the health, law and finance articles, where readers would normally expect to know who is giving the information.

Not the same as Safetrode Industries

Searches for the name also bring up Safetrode, an earthing and lightning-protection supplier based in Tamil Nadu. The overlap is not a coincidence of spelling. The domain itself changed hands:

  1. February 2019: safetrode.com was registered, according to the public RDAP domain record. The current registration runs to 2032 and was last changed in April 2025.
  2. December 2021: an Internet Archive snapshot shows the site of a “Chemical Earthing Company Chennai”, selling earthing electrodes and ESE lightning arresters.
  3. April 2022: the same address had become a WordPress blog with the tagline “Daily Enjoyment with Daily News”, covering fashion, travel and real estate.
  4. 2025 onwards: the current “Safe Trode” blog appears, with its oldest surviving post from January 2025.

We found no evidence that the present blog is connected to the earthing business. If you are looking for earthing products or the Chennai company, safetrode.com is no longer its website.

Why a blog would buy an old company domain

Reusing an established domain is a common practice in online publishing. An address that a business used for years may still have links pointing to it from directories, suppliers and other sites. Search engines treat links as one signal of trust, so an older domain can help new content appear in results sooner.

Google’s spam policies call one version of this “expired domain abuse”: buying an expired domain and using it mainly to host low-value content that benefits from the old site’s reputation. Whether a particular site crosses that line is a judgement for Google, and we are not making it here. Our guide to Google’s link spam rules explains how these policies work.

Is safetrode.com safe?

Reading it: low risk. The site loads over HTTPS, is served through Cloudflare and does not ask readers to register, pay or download anything. We did not find requests for personal or banking information.

Acting on it: be careful. Several posts are written about, or in support of, specific businesses. They include an online casino recommended to Canadian players, a guide to buying Bitcoin with Canadian dollars, a post about a VPN brand and an article quoting a named insurance agency. These posts are not labelled as sponsored, but their wording reads like promotion.

For readers in India, two points apply:

  • Online gambling: real-money online gaming has been banned in India since the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025. Offshore casinos promoted on foreign blogs are not legal options.
  • Crypto: crypto purchases are taxed at 30% on gains, with 1% TDS on transfers, and carry high price risk. A blog post is not a substitute for checking that an exchange is registered with India’s Financial Intelligence Unit.

How to judge a site like this

Safetrode.com is one of many general blogs that publish a broad mix of articles, take advertising and sponsored posts, and rank for their own names. We have looked at similar sites, including Tozymagazine.com and SpiritualHubz.com. The same checks work for any of them:

  1. Look for a named owner. A real editor, company or address makes a site accountable.
  2. Check the author. One account writing on medicine, law and crypto is a sign of a content site, not a specialist publication.
  3. Spot the sales pitch. Posts that name one brand and link to it repeatedly are usually paid placements.
  4. Verify the numbers. Funding figures, fees and legal requirements should be checked against official sources or original news reports.
  5. Check the domain history. The Internet Archive’s Wayback Machine shows what a site used to be.

Frequently asked questions

What is safetrode.com? A general-interest WordPress blog called “Safe Trode”, publishing articles on technology, finance, business, law and lifestyle topics.

Is safetrode.com a trading or investment platform? No. Despite one post about futures trading, the site offers no trading, broking or investment services.

Is safetrode.com related to Safetrode earthing products? The domain was used by a Chennai earthing company around 2021. The current blog shows no connection to that business.

Who owns safetrode.com? The site does not say. Domain ownership details are not public, and all posts appear under the name “Kevin”.

Does safetrode.com accept contributed articles? The site has no page inviting contributors, but its contact page invites advertising and partnership enquiries by email.

This article is based on safetrode.com’s public pages and WordPress data, the domain’s RDAP record and Internet Archive snapshots, all checked on 21 November 2026. Buisinesstoday has no connection with safetrode.com or with Safetrode Industries.
